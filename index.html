How to Read the Flow

Use the options flow to see where the "smart money" is moving. Here's what to look for:

  • BULLISH:

    Aggressive buying of Calls at the Ask price, or selling of Puts at the Bid price.

  • BEARISH:

    Aggressive buying of Puts at the Ask price, or selling of Calls at the Bid price.

  • Premium:

    The total dollar amount of the trade. Large premiums (e.g., >$100k) signify higher conviction or institutional activity.
